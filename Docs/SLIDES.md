## Slide 5 — E5: NFL collapse (light)

**Title (32pt):** What happens when labels are randomized?

**Sub (Consolas):** `E5  |  10 label derangements  |  zero fixed points  |  the paper never tested this`

**Left card (tint fill):** True labels → **F₁ = 0.613** (primary, 46pt)
Caption: Zero-Shot Gemini @ n=160

**Arrow `→`** (red, large, between cards)

**Right card (light red fill):** Deranged labels → **F₁ = 0.032** (red, 46pt)
Caption: Same docs, permuted GT (π(y) ≠ y)

**Bottom line:** `94.8% collapse` (red, bold) — performance drops to random chance
when concept-label alignment is broken. The paper's gains are real, not a measurement artifact.
